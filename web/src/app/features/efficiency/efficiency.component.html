How prompt caching saves you money

Most of every Claude Code prompt — system prompt, tool definitions, CLAUDE.md, prior conversation — gets cached. Cached reads cost about 10% of fresh input, so the hit ratio above is the share of your prompt being served at that discount.

To improve your hit rate:

  • Prefer long sessions over many short ones — every new session starts cold and pays the cache-write premium fresh.
  • Keep CLAUDE.md and project-level context stable — edits invalidate every cached prefix that follows them.
  • Use /clear deliberately — it resets the cache.
